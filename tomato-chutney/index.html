Tomato Chutney

Minija

 

A tangy, spicy tomato chutney cooked with shallots, garlic, and red chillies, ideal for dosa and idli.

Easy • 20 mins • Chutney • Veg • Serve warm or at room temperature

Ingredients

  • 3–4 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 6–8 small shallots, or 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3–5 dried red chillies, adjusted to taste
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder, for colour
  • 1 small piece of tamarind (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil or other cooking oil

For tempering

  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 1 dried red chilli

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pan.
  2. Add the dried red chillies, garlic, and shallots. Sauté until softened and lightly golden.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and salt. Cook until the tomatoes become mushy.
  4. Add the Kashmiri chilli powder and mix well.
  5. Continue cooking until the mixture thickens and the oil begins to separate.
  6. Add the tamarind, if using, and cook for another 1–2 minutes.
  7. Switch off the heat and allow the mixture to cool.
  8. Grind into a smooth or slightly coarse chutney.
  9. For the tempering, heat a little oil in a small pan. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.
  10. Add the curry leaves and dried red chilli. Fry briefly and pour over the chutney.

Notes

  • Kashmiri chilli powder adds colour without making the chutney excessively spicy.
  • Omit the tamarind if the tomatoes are already sufficiently tangy.
  • Serve with dosa, idli
