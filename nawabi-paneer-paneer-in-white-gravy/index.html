Nawabi Paneer (Paneer in White Gravy)

Minija

 

A luxurious paneer curry with a delicate white gravy made from nuts, yoghurt, milk, and aromatic whole spices.

Medium • 40–50 mins • Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

For the onion paste

  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 green chillies

For the nut paste

  • 10 cashew nuts
  • 10 almonds (optional)
  • 2 tsp poppy seeds (optional)
  • Hot water, for soaking

For the curry

  • 300 g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp ghee
  • 1 inch cinnamon stick
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 star anise
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ cup yoghurt (curd)
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1½ cups milk
  • ¼ cup fresh cream
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Saffron strands (optional)

Healthier alternative

  • Puréed tofu instead of the milk and fresh cream

Method

Prepare the nut paste

  1. Soak the cashews, almonds, and poppy seeds, if using, in hot water for about 1 hour.
  2. Drain and grind them into a fine paste. Set aside.

Prepare the onion paste

  1. Grind the onions, ginger, garlic, and green chillies into a smooth paste.

Cook the gravy

  1. Heat the oil and ghee in a pan.
  2. Add the cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, bay leaf, star anise, and cumin seeds. Sauté briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add the onion paste and cook until the raw smell disappears and the paste is lightly cooked.
  4. Add the nut paste and salt. Mix well.
  5. Lower the heat and gradually add the yoghurt, stirring continuously to prevent curdling.
  6. Cook for about 5 minutes.
  7. Add the milk and simmer for another 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Add the paneer

  1. Add the paneer cubes and mix gently.
  2. Cover and cook over low heat for about 5–10 minutes, until the paneer is heated through and the gravy reaches your preferred consistency.
  3. Stir in the fresh cream and crushed kasuri methi.
  4. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves and saffron strands, if desired.

Notes

  • You can use about ½ tsp garam masala instead of the whole spices if you prefer a simpler preparation.
  • Add the yoghurt gradually over low heat to help prevent curdling.
  • For a healthier alternative, use puréed tofu in place of the milk and cream, adjusting the consistency with water as needed.
  • Serve with naan, roti, chapati, or pulao.
