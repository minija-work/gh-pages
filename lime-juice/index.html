Lime Juice 🍋

Minija

A refreshing lime drink with a hint of ginger, cardamom and coconut — a delightful combination of sweet, tangy and aromatic flavours!

Easy • 10 mins • Drink • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 lime
  • 1 small piece of ginger
  • 1 cardamom pod
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tsp grated coconut
  • Ice cubes, as needed

Method

  1. Add the sugar and 1 cup of water to a blender and blend until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the ginger, cardamom and grated coconut. Blend well.
  3. Add the remaining 1 cup of water and blend again.
  4. Squeeze the lime juice into the mixture and blend briefly.
  5. Strain the mixture through a sieve.
  6. Pour into glasses, add ice cubes and serve chilled.

Note

  • Adjust the sugar according to your taste.
  • Blend briefly after adding the lime juice to preserve its fresh flavour.
  • Serve immediately for the best taste and aroma.
