Butter Paneer Masala

Minija

Soft paneer cubes simmered in a rich, creamy tomato and cashew gravy with aromatic Indian spices.

Easy • 35–40 mins • Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • 200–250 g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter, plus a little extra for garnish
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped, or 1 cup tomato purée
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 10–12 cashew nuts, soaked
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 cup water, or as needed
  • ½ cup fresh cream or milk
  • Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), for garnish
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped, for garnish
  • A pinch of cumin seeds (optional)
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander stems (optional)

Healthier alternative

  • Puréed soft tofu instead of cream
  • Alternatively, blend one paneer cube with a little milk to make a creamy sauce

Method

Prepare the base paste

  1. Heat a little oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds, if using.
  2. Add the chopped onions and sauté until soft.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook until mushy.
  4. Add the soaked cashews and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  5. Allow the mixture to cool, then blend into a smooth paste.

Prepare the gravy

  1. Heat the butter and remaining oil in the same pan.
  2. Add the ginger-garlic paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  3. Add the red chilli powder and stir briefly over low heat, taking care not to burn it.
  4. Add the blended paste and mix well.
  5. Add the water and simmer for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Add the paneer

  1. Gently add the paneer cubes and cook for 3–5 minutes.Add the garam masala and mix gently.
  2. Stir in the cream or puréed tofu.
  3. Finish with a little butter, crushed kasuri methi, and chopped coriander leaves.

Notes

  • Add the chopped coriander stems while sautéing the onions for extra flavour, if desired.
  • Avoid overcooking the paneer, as it can become firm.
  • For a lighter version, use puréed tofu instead of cream.
  • Serve with naan, roti, chapati, or jeera rice.
