Split Green Gram Payasam (Moong Dal Payasam)

Minija

 

A traditional, rich payasam made with roasted split green gram, jaggery, coconut milk, and aromatic spices.

Easy • 40–50 mins • Dessert • Veg • Serve warm

Ingredients

  • 1 cup split green gram (moong dal)
  • 4 cups lukewarm water, for cooking the dal
  • 300–400 g jaggery (adjust to taste)
  • 1 cup water, for melting the jaggery
  • ½ cup thick coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp coconut milk, to add to the cooking water
  • 2–3 tbsp ghee
  • 10 cashew nuts
  • 10 raisins
  • ½ tsp cardamom powder
  • ¼ tsp dry ginger powder

Method

Cook the dal

  1. Dry-roast the split green gram in a pan over medium-low heat until the raw smell disappears. Do not let it turn brown.
  2. Transfer the roasted dal to a pressure cooker.
  3. Add the 4 cups of water and 1 tbsp coconut milk.
  4. Pressure-cook until the first whistle, then cook for another 5 minutes over low heat.
  5. Switch off the heat and allow the pressure to release naturally.
  6. Open the cooker and check that the dal is soft.

Prepare the jaggery

  1. Melt the jaggery in 1 cup water over low heat.
  2. Strain the melted jaggery syrup to remove any impurities.
  3. Add the jaggery syrup to the cooked dal.
  4. Simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring gently.

Finish the payasam

  1. Reduce the heat and stir in the thick coconut milk.
  2. Add the cardamom powder and dry ginger powder. Mix well.
  3. Heat the ghee in a small pan.
  4. Fry the cashews until golden, then add the raisins and fry until they puff up.
  5. Pour the fried nuts, raisins, and ghee into the payasam. Mix gently and serve warm.

Notes

  • Do not brown the dal while roasting, as this can change the flavour.
  • Strain the jaggery syrup before adding it to the dal.
  • After adding the thick coconut milk, heat gently and avoid vigorous boiling.
  • Adjust the jaggery according to your preferred sweetness.
