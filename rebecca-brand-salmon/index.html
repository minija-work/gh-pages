Rebecca Brand Salmon

Minija

 

Sweet and savory baked salmon with a simple glaze.

Easy • 15 mins • Non-Veg • Main Dish • Oven-Grilled

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tsp soya sauce
  • Black pepper powder
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Lemon slices (for serving)

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to .220°C.
  2. Line a baking pan with foil.
  3. Marinate the salmon with brown sugar, soya sauce, salt, and black pepper. Add a little olive oil.
  4. Place the salmon on the prepared pan.
  5. Grill for about 8 minutes or until cooked through.
  6. Serve with sliced lemon.

Notes

  • Adjust sugar and soya sauce to taste depending on how sweet or salty you prefer.
  • Cooking time may vary slightly depending on the thickness of the salmon.
