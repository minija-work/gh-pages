Palak Paneer Dosa

Minija

 

Ingredients

For the palak dosa batter

  • 2 cups dosa batter
  • 1 cup fresh spinach (palak)

For the paneer filling

  • 200 g paneer, crumbled or grated
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped 1
  • tbs chickpea flour
  • ½ tsp Fenugreek leaf powder(Kasoori methi powder)
  • ½ tsp Cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 Green chilli chopped
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp chilli powder, to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil or butter
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tbs chopped ginger
  • 1 tbs chopped garlic

Method

  1. Prepare the palak puree. Wash the spinach thoroughly. Blanch it in hot water for 1–2 minutes, then cool and blend..
  2. Prepare the dosa batter. Mix the palak puree into the dosa batter. Add salt if needed and a little water to achieve a spreadable consistency.
  3. Prepare the paneer filling. Heat oil or butter in a pan. Sputter mustard seeds and cumin seeds.Add chopped onions and sauté until soft.
  4. Add ginger-garlic ,chick pea flour,chopped green chillies and cook briefly.
  5. Add turmeric powder, chilli powder, Fenugreek leaf powder, garam masala powder and salt. Mix well.
  6. Add crumbled paneer and cook for 2–3 minutes. Sprinkle some water and cook till the water evaporates.
  7. Make the dosa. Heat a dosa tawa and lightly grease it. Pour a ladleful of palak batter and spread into a thin circle.
  8. Drizzle a little oil or ghee around the edges. Cook until the underside is crisp and cooked through.
  9. Place the paneer filling in the centre. Fold the dosa and serve hot.

Notes

  • Adjust the green chillies to your preferred spice level.
  • Add grated cheese to the paneer filling for a richer taste.
  • Serve with coconut chutney or tomato chutney.
  • For a brighter green colour, avoid overcooking the spinach before blending.
