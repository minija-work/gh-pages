Spicy Potato Fry

Minija

 

A simple and spicy potato fry with garlic, red chilli powder and curry leaves. A delicious accompaniment to rice, dal curry or curd rice.

Easy • 20–25 mins • Side Dish • Veg

Ingredients

  • 3 large potatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ¾ tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • A few curry leaves

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into small, even-sized cubes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  4. Add the crushed garlic and curry leaves. Sauté until the raw smell of the garlic disappears.
  5. Add the chopped potatoes and fry for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Add the salt, turmeric powder and red chilli powder. Mix well so the spices coat the potatoes evenly.
  7. Continue frying over medium-low heat until the potatoes are cooked through and the chilli powder is roasted without burning.
  8. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. Switch off the heat once the potatoes are tender but still hold their shape.

Notes

  • Cut the potatoes into small, even cubes so they cook uniformly.
  • Adjust the red chilli powder according to your preferred spice level.
  • Avoid overcooking or stirring too vigorously, as the potatoes may break apart.
  • For a crispier finish, cook uncovered over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally.
