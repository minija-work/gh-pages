Spiced Buttermilk 🌿

Minija

A refreshing and savoury buttermilk flavoured with ginger, green chilli, shallots and curry leaves — a perfect cooling drink for hot days!

Easy • 10 mins • Drink • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 small piece of ginger, chopped
  • 1 large green chilli (adjust according to your spice level)
  • 4–5 small onions (shallots)
  • 2 lemon leaves (optional)
  • ½ litre curd
  • Salt, to taste
  • A few curry leaves, chopped
  • 3 cups water

Method

  1. Crush the ginger, green chilli and shallots in the small jar of a mixer.
  2. Add the curd to a large bowl and whisk until smooth.
  3. Add the crushed ingredients and chopped curry leaves to the curd.
  4. Add salt to taste and mix well.
  5. Gradually add the water while whisking until the buttermilk reaches your desired consistency.
  6. Serve chilled or at room temperature.

Note

  • Adjust the green chilli and salt according to your taste.
  • For a more refreshing flavour, chill the buttermilk before serving.
  • Lemon leaves are optional but add a lovely aroma.
