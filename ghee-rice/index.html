Ghee Rice

Minija

Fragrant Kerala-style rice cooked in ghee with cashews and raisins.

Easy • 25 mins • Veg • Main Dish

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 8–10 cashew nuts
  • 1 tbsp raisins
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • 2 cardamom pods
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 cups water
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Wash basmati rice and soak for 15–20 minutes. Drain well.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp ghee in a pan. Fry cashews until golden, then add raisins. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, add remaining ghee. Add cloves, cinnamon, cardamom, and bay leaf. Add sliced onions and sauté until lightly golden.
  4. Add drained rice and gently fry for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add water and salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low heat until rice is done.
  6. Fluff gently and top with fried cashews and raisins. Serve warm.

Notes

  •       Serve it with chicken curry
