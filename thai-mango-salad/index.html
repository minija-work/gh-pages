Thai Mango Salad 🥭

Minija

 

A tangy and spicy raw mango salad with shallots, fresh red chillies and crunchy roasted peanuts.

Easy • 10 mins • Salad • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 raw mango, peeled and cut into thin strips
  • 3 shallots, chopped
  • 2 fresh red chillies, finely chopped
  • A few roasted peanuts
  • Chopped coriander leaves, for garnish
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp sugar

Method

  1. Place the thinly sliced raw mango in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the chopped shallots and red chillies.
  3. Add the salt and sugar.
  4. Mix everything together until well combined.
  5. Garnish with roasted peanuts and chopped coriander leaves.
  6. Serve immediately.

Note

  • Choose a firm, sour raw mango for the best flavour.
  • Adjust the sugar, salt and chillies according to your taste.
  • Add the roasted peanuts just before serving to retain their crunch.
