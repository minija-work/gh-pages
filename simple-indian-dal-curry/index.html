Simple Indian Dal Curry

Minija

A comforting everyday dal made with lentils, onions, tomatoes and mild spices. Perfect with rice, chapati or dosa.

Easy • 30 mins • Main Dish • Veg

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (yellow split pigeon peas)
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 medium tomato, chopped
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 cups water, or as needed
  • 1–2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves

For Tempering

  • 1 tsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • A pinch of asafoetida (optional)

Method

  1. Wash the toor dal thoroughly. Add it to a pressure cooker with turmeric powder and 3 cups of water.
  2. Pressure-cook until the dal is soft and easy to mash, usually for 3–5 whistles depending on the cooker.
  3. Heat 1–2 tablespoons of oil in a pan. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft and lightly golden.
  4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  5. Add the chopped tomato, red chilli powder, cumin powder and coriander powder. Cook until the tomatoes soften and become pulpy.
  6. Add the cooked dal and salt. Mix well, mashing the dal slightly.
  7. Add water as needed to achieve your preferred consistency. Simmer for 5–10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. For the tempering, heat oil or ghee in a small pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  9. Add cumin seeds, dried red chillies, curry leaves and asafoetida, if using. Fry briefly without burning.
  10. Pour the tempering over the dal and mix gently.
  11. Garnish with chopped coriander leaves and serve hot.

Notes

  • You can use moong dal or masoor dal instead of toor dal.
  • For a richer flavour, use ghee for the tempering.
  • Adjust the water depending on whether you prefer thick dal for chapati or a thinner curry for rice.
  • A squeeze of lemon juice just before serving is optional.
