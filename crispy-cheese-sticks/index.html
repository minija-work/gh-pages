Crispy Cheese Sticks 🧀

Minija

 

Golden, crunchy cheese sticks with a deliciously melted mozzarella centre. Perfect as a party snack or an evening treat, served hot with tomato ketchup or marinara sauce.

Easy • 20 minutes preparation + 1–2 hours freezing • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Mozzarella cheese – block or string cheese, cut into finger-sized sticks
  • All-purpose flour – 1 cup
  • Eggs – 2, beaten
  • Breadcrumbs – 1½ cups (panko preferred for extra crunch)
  • Italian seasoning – 1 tsp (optional)
  • Garlic powder – ½ tsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Oil – for deep frying

Method

1. Cut the cheese

  1. Cut the mozzarella into thick, finger-sized sticks.

2. Prepare the coating stations

  1. In the first bowl, mix the flour with a little salt.
  2. In the second bowl, beat the eggs.
  3. In the third bowl, combine the breadcrumbs, Italian seasoning, and garlic powder.

3. Coat the cheese sticks

  1. Roll each cheese stick in the flour, shaking off any excess.
  2. Dip it into the beaten egg.
  3. Coat it thoroughly with the breadcrumb mixture.
  4. Dip it in the egg again and coat with breadcrumbs a second time. Press gently to help the crumbs stick.
  5. Repeat with all the cheese sticks, ensuring there are no uncovered areas.

4. Freeze

  1. Arrange the coated cheese sticks in a single layer on a tray.
  2. Freeze for at least 1–2 hours, until firm.

Cooking Method 1: Deep Frying

  1. Heat oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  2. Fry the cheese sticks straight from the freezer for approximately 1–2 minutes, until golden brown.
  3. Fry in small batches to avoid overcrowding the pan.
  4. Remove immediately when golden and place on kitchen paper to drain excess oil.

Cooking Method 2: Air Frying

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Lightly spray the air-fryer basket and frozen cheese sticks with oil.
  3. Arrange the sticks in a single layer without overcrowding.
  4. Air fry for 6–8 minutes, turning halfway through.
  5. Check after 5 minutes, as the cheese can melt and leak out quickly.

Tips for Perfect Cheese Sticks

  • Always cook directly from frozen. This helps the coating crisp up before the cheese melts too much.
  • Double coating is essential. It helps prevent the cheese from leaking during cooking.
  • Keep the oil at a moderate temperature. Oil that is too hot can brown the coating before the cheese is ready, while oil that is too cool can make the coating greasy.
  • Do not overcook; remove the sticks as soon as they turn golden.
  • Serve immediately for the best crispy texture and melted cheese centre.

Notes

  • Serve hot with tomato ketchup, marinara sauce, garlic mayonnaise, or your favourite dipping sauce.
