Coconut Chutney with Red Chillies

Minija

 

A traditional coconut chutney with dried red chillies, shallots, and tamarind for a spicy, tangy flavour.

Easy • 10 mins • Chutney • Veg • Serve fresh

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grated coconut, preferably fresh
  • 3–5 dried red chillies, adjusted to taste
  • 2–3 small shallots, or 1 tbsp chopped onion
  • 1 small piece of tamarind, or ½ tsp tamarind paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Water, as needed

For tempering

  • 1 tbsp coconut oil or other cooking oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1–2 dried red chillies
  • A few curry leaves

Method

  1. Add the grated coconut, dried red chillies, shallots, tamarind, and salt to a grinder.
  2. Add a little water and grind into a smooth or slightly coarse paste.
  3. Transfer the chutney to a serving bowl.
  4. Heat the coconut oil in a small pan.
  5. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.
  6. Add the dried red chillies and curry leaves. Fry briefly.
  7. Pour the tempering over the chutney and mix gently.

Notes

  • Adjust the number of dried red chillies according to your preferred spice level.
  • Use tamarind sparingly and adjust to taste.
  • Serve with dosa, idli, or steamed rice.
