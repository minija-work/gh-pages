Aloo Paratha 🥔

Minija

A delicious Indian flatbread stuffed with spiced mashed potatoes, cooked on a tawa until golden and crispy. Best served hot with mint chutney, curd, or pickle.

Easy • 40–50 minutes • Vegetarian • Serves 4–5

Ingredients

For the dough

  • Whole-wheat flour – 2 cups
  • Ajwain (carom seeds) – ½ tsp (optional)
  • Salt – to taste
  • Water – as needed
  • Oil – for cooking

For the potato filling

  • Potatoes – 4 medium
  • Green chillies – 2, finely chopped
  • Coriander leaves – a handful, chopped
  • Kashmiri red chilli powder – 1 tsp
  • Garam masala powder – ½ tsp
  • Chaat masala powder – ½ tsp (optional)
  • Salt – to taste

Method

1. Prepare the dough

  1. Combine the whole-wheat flour, ajwain, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add water gradually and knead until you get a soft, smooth dough.
  3. Cover and let the dough rest while you prepare the filling.

2. Prepare the potato filling

  1. Boil the potatoes until tender.
  2. Allow them to cool slightly, peel them, and mash them well.
  3. Add the chopped green chillies, coriander leaves, red chilli powder, garam masala, chaat masala, and salt.
  4. Mix thoroughly until all the ingredients are evenly combined. Make sure there are no large lumps.

3. Stuff the parathas

  1. Divide the dough into equal-sized balls.
  2. Flatten each ball into a small disc.
  3. Place a portion of the potato filling in the centre.
  4. Bring the edges together and seal the filling inside, forming a parcel.
  5. Gently flatten the parcel and roll it out into a round paratha. Dust lightly with flour if needed.
  6. Repeat with the remaining dough balls and filling.

4. Cook the parathas

  1. Heat a tawa over medium heat.
  2. Place a rolled paratha on the hot tawa and cook until the underside develops light brown spots.
  3. Flip and cook the other side.
  4. Apply a little oil around the edges and on both sides.
  5. Continue cooking, flipping as needed, until the paratha is golden brown and cooked through.
  6. Repeat with the remaining parathas.

Notes

  • Mash the potatoes thoroughly to prevent the filling from tearing the dough.
  • Let the potatoes cool before stuffing the parathas.
  • Roll gently and evenly so the filling does not come out.
  • Cook over medium heat to ensure the paratha cooks through without burning.
  • Serve hot with mint chutney, plain yoghurt or curd, mango pickle, and a cup of Indian tea.
