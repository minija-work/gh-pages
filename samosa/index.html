Samosa 🥟

Minija

 

A popular Indian snack with a crispy, golden pastry filled with spiced potatoes, green peas and aromatic Indian spices. Perfect for tea time or as a party snack!

Medium • 60 mins • Snack • Vegetarian

Ingredients

For the Dough

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • 4 tbsp oil or ghee
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Water, as needed for kneading

For the Filling

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • ½ cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • A little lemon juice or amchur (dry mango powder), optional
  • Oil, for deep-frying

Method

1. Make the Dough

  1. Mix the flour and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Add the oil or ghee and rub it into the flour until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
  3. Add water gradually and knead into a firm, stiff dough. Do not make it too soft.
  4. Cover and let the dough rest for 20–30 minutes.

2. Prepare the Filling

  1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan.
  2. Add the cumin seeds and allow them to crackle.
  3. Add the chopped onion, green chillies and ginger. Sauté until the onion softens.
  4. Add the green peas and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add the turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder and salt. Mix well.
  6. Add the mashed potatoes and combine thoroughly.
  7. Stir in the garam masala, chopped coriander leaves and lemon juice or amchur, if using.
  8. Remove from the heat and allow the filling to cool completely.

3. Shape the Samosas

  1. Divide the dough into small, equal-sized balls.
  2. Roll each ball into an oval shape.
  3. Cut the oval in half to make two semicircles.
  4. Take one semicircle and form a cone, sealing the straight edge with a little water.
  5. Fill the cone with the cooled potato mixture.
  6. Apply a little water to the edges and seal the top firmly.
  7. Repeat with the remaining dough and filling.

4. Fry the Samosas

  1. Heat oil in a deep pan over low to medium heat.
  2. Carefully add the samosas to the oil without overcrowding the pan.
  3. Fry slowly until they turn golden brown and crispy, turning occasionally for even cooking.
  4. Remove and drain on kitchen paper.
  5. Serve hot with mint chutney or tamarind chutney.

Tips for Perfect Samosas

  • Firm dough: A stiff dough helps create a crispy pastry.
  • Cool filling: Always let the potato filling cool completely before stuffing.
  • Slow frying: Start frying at a lower temperature so the pastry cooks evenly and becomes crisp.
  • Seal properly: Ensure all edges are tightly sealed to prevent the filling from escaping.
  • Air-frying option: Brush the samosas with oil and air-fry at 180°C for approximately 15–18 minutes, turning halfway through. Cooking time may vary by air fryer.
