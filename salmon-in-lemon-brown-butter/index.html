Salmon in Lemon Brown Butter

Minija

 

Ingredients

  • 1¼ lb (about 570 g) skinless, boneless salmon fillets, cut into 4 portions (about 5 oz or 140 g each, approximately 1 inch thick)
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ⅛ tsp black pepper
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp grated lemon zest
  • 4 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice (from about 2 lemons)

Method

  1. Season the salmon fillets with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and melt the butter.
  3. Continue cooking the butter until it turns lightly golden and smells nutty. Watch carefully to prevent it from burning.
  4. Add the salmon fillets to the pan.
  5. Cook for 3–4 minutes on the first side, then turn and cook for another 2–3 minutes, depending on the thickness.
  6. Add the lemon zest and pour in the lemon juice.
  7. Cook for another minute, spooning the lemon butter sauce over the salmon.
  8. Transfer the salmon to a serving dish and pour the remaining sauce over the fillets.

Notes

  • Cooking time depends on the thickness of the salmon. Ensure it is cooked through; a food thermometer should read 63°C at the thickest part.
  • Serve immediately with vegetables, rice, or mashed potatoes.
