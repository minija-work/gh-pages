Onion and Peanut Salad 🥜

Minija

A crunchy and flavourful salad combining fresh onions, green chillies and roasted peanuts with a touch of coconut oil.

Easy • 10 mins • Salad • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 2 medium onions, chopped
  • 3 green chillies, finely chopped
  • Coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • ½ cup dry-roasted peanuts

Method

  1. Add the chopped onions and green chillies to a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the chopped coriander leaves.
  3. Add the coconut oil, salt and sugar.
  4. Add the dry-roasted peanuts.
  5. Mix everything together until well combined.
  6. Serve immediately.

Note

  • Adjust the green chillies according to your spice preference.
  • Add the roasted peanuts just before serving to maintain their crunch.
