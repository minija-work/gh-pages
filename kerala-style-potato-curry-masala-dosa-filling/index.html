Kerala-Style Potato Curry (Masala Dosa Filling)

Minija

 

A soft, mildly spiced potato filling made with onions, green chillies, ginger, and curry leaves for classic masala dosa.

Easy • 30 mins • Breakfast / Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • 3–4 medium potatoes, boiled and roughly mashed
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 1–2 green chillies, thinly sliced or chopped
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal (optional)
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped ginger
  • 2 tbsp oil, preferably coconut oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 1–1½ cups water, as needed
  • Fresh coriander leaves (optional)
  • ½ tsp lemon juice (optional)

Method

Prepare the tempering: Heat the oil in a pan. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.

  1. Add the urad dal, if using, and fry until lightly golden.
  2. Add the curry leaves and green chillies. Fry briefly.
  3. Add the sliced onions and chopped ginger. Sauté until the onions soften without browning.
  4. Add the turmeric powder and salt. Mix well.
  5. Pour in 1 cup of water and bring to a boil.
  6. Add the mashed potatoes and mix thoroughly. Add more water if needed.
  7. Simmer for 3–5 minutes until the curry is soft, slightly thick, and spreadable.
  8. Add chopped coriander leaves and lemon juice, if desired. Mix well and switch off the heat.
  9. Spread the potato filling inside a crispy dosa, fold, and serve hot.

Notes

  • Mash the potatoes roughly for a traditional texture rather than making them completely smooth.
  • Adjust the water to get a moist filling that is easy to spread but not runny.
  • Coconut oil gives the filling a traditional Kerala flavour.
  • Serve with coconut chutney and sambar for a complete masala dosa meal
