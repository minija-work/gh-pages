Green Tea Pudding

Minija

 

A delicate, creamy milk pudding flavoured with matcha green tea.

Easy • 20 mins preparation • 2–3 hrs chilling • Dessert • Veg • Chilled

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • ½ cup water, for soaking the china grass
  • 8–10 g china grass (agar-agar)
  • 4–5 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1–1½ tsp matcha (green tea powder)
  • 2 tbsp condensed milk (optional, for creaminess)

Method

Prepare the china grass

  1. Cut the china grass into small pieces, if necessary, and soak it in ½ cup water for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Heat gently until fully dissolved, ensuring no strands remain.

Prepare the matcha

  1. Place the matcha powder in a small bowl.
  2. Add 2–3 tbsp warm milk and whisk until smooth and free of lumps.

Prepare the milk mixture

  1. Heat the milk, sugar, and condensed milk in a saucepan.
  2. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Avoid boiling vigorously.
  3. Add the dissolved china grass to the hot milk and stir continuously for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add the matcha mixture and stir until evenly combined.

Set the pudding

  1. Pour into individual moulds or a shallow tray.
  2. Allow it to cool at room temperature.
  3. Refrigerate for 2–3 hours, or until firm.

Notes

  • Adjust the amount of matcha according to your preferred green tea flavour.
  • Whisk the matcha with warm milk before adding it to prevent lumps.
  • Agar-agar powder can be used instead of china grass; follow the package instructions.
