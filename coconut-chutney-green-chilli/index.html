Coconut Chutney (Green Chilli)

Minija

Fresh coconut chutney blended with green chillies and tempered spices.

Easy • 10 mins • Veg • Chutney • Side Dish

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup grated coconut
  • A small piece of ginger
  • 4 green chillies
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed
  •  tempering
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 3–4 dry red chillies
  • A few curry leaves

Method

  1. Grind grated coconut, ginger, green chillies, lime juice, salt, and a little water into a smooth paste.
  2. Transfer the chutney to a bowl.
  3. Heat oil in a small pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  4. Add dry red chillies and curry leaves, and fry briefly.
  5. Pour the tempering over the chutney and mix lightly.
  6. Serve fresh.

Notes

  • Adjust green chillies based on spice preference.
  • Add a little more water to get your desired chutney consistency.
