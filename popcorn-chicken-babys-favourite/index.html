Popcorn Chicken (Baby’s Favourite) 🍗

Minija

 

Crispy, bite-sized chicken pieces coated in crunchy breadcrumbs and flavoured with soy sauce and chilli flakes. A delicious homemade snack that’s perfect for parties, movie nights or tea time!

Easy • 30 mins • Snack • Non-Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 250 g boneless chicken, cut into small pieces
  • Oil, for deep-frying
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • 1 tsp red chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp cornflour (cornstarch)
  • 1½ cups dried breadcrumbs

Method

  1. Heat sufficient oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  2. Place the chicken pieces in a mixing bowl.
  3. Add the turmeric powder, salt, red chilli flakes, soy sauce and egg. Mix well until the chicken is evenly coated.
  4. Add the cornflour and mix thoroughly.
  5. Place the breadcrumbs in a separate bowl.
  6. Coat each chicken piece with breadcrumbs, pressing gently so they stick well.
  7. Carefully place the coated chicken pieces into the hot oil in small batches.
  8. Deep-fry until golden brown and crispy, turning occasionally for even cooking.
  9. Remove the chicken and drain on absorbent paper.
  10. Serve hot with tomato ketchup, mayonnaise or your favourite dipping sauce.

Note

  • Cut the chicken into small, even-sized pieces for uniform cooking.
  • Avoid overcrowding the pan, as this lowers the oil temperature and can make the coating soggy.
  • Make sure the chicken is fully cooked inside before serving.
  • For extra crispiness, double-coat the chicken with breadcrumbs before frying.
