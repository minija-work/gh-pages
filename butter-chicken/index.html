Butter Chicken

Minija

Tender marinated chicken simmered in a rich, creamy tomato gravy with butter, cashews, and aromatic Indian spices.

Medium • 60–75 mins • Main Course • Non-Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

For the marinade

  • 500 g boneless chicken, preferably thighs
  • ½ cup thick yoghurt (curd)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste

For the gravy

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2–3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 10–12 cashew nuts, soaked and ground into a paste
  • 1½ tsp red chilli powder, or to taste
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed

For finishing

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), crushed
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

Method

Marinate the chicken

  1. In a bowl, combine the chicken with the yoghurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, red chilli powder, garam masala, lemon juice, and salt.
  2. Mix well, cover, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Marinating overnight gives a deeper flavour.

Cook the chicken

  1. Grill, pan-fry, or air-fry the marinated chicken until lightly charred and about 80–90% cooked.
  2. Set aside.

Prepare the gravy

  1. Heat the oil and 2 tbsp butter in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped onion and sauté until golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate.
  5. Add the soaked cashew paste and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Add the red chilli powder, coriander powder, and salt. Mix well.
  7. Allow the mixture to cool slightly, then blend until smooth.
  8. Return the blended gravy to the pan. Add a little water if it is too thick, adjusting to your preferred consistency.

Combine and finish

  1. Add the cooked chicken pieces to the gravy.
  2. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and has absorbed the flavours.
  3. Stir in the cream, optional sugar, and garam masala.
  4. Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and add it to the gravy.
  5. Add the remaining 1 tbsp butter and mix gently.
  6. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot.

Notes

  • Lightly charring the chicken adds a smoky, restaurant-style flavour.
  • Adjust the chilli powder and cream according to your taste.
  • Sugar is optional but can help balance the acidity of the tomatoes.
  • Serve with naan, roti, chapati, or basmati rice.
