 Thai Green Curry Rice 🌿

Minija

A fragrant rice dish made with homemade green curry paste, coconut milk, vegetables, and fresh basil.

Medium • 35–45 minutes • Vegetarian

Ingredients

For the green curry paste

  • Coriander stems – ¼ cup
  • Garlic – 5 cloves
  • Shallots – 5
  • Green chillies – 2
  • Ginger – 1 inch
  • Lemongrass – 2 stalks, chopped
  • Fresh basil leaves – 10–20
  • Lime zest – ½ lime
  • Black pepper powder – 1 tsp
  • Coriander powder – 1 tsp
  • Cumin powder – 1 tsp
  • Water – ¼ cup
  • Oil – 1 tbsp

For the rice

  • Basmati rice – 1 cup
  • Onion – 1, chopped
  • Carrot – ¼ cup, chopped
  • Green beans – ¼ cup, chopped
  • Tofu – ¼ cup, cubed
  • Soy sauce – 1 tsp
  • Coconut milk – ½ cup
  • Water – enough to make 1¼ cups of liquid with the coconut milk
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh basil – for garnish

Method

  1. Blend all the green curry paste ingredients into a smooth paste.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the paste until fragrant.
  3. Add the soy sauce, carrot, green beans, and tofu. Cook for about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the coconut milk and enough water to make 1¼ cups of liquid in total.
  5. Add the washed rice and salt. Mix well.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice is tender and the liquid is absorbed. Alternatively, cook in a pressure cooker for one whistle, adjusting the method to suit your cooker.
  7. Garnish with fresh basil and serve hot.

Notes

  • Adjust the green chillies according to your preferred spice level.
  • You can substitute other vegetables for the carrot and green beans.
  • Stir gently to avoid breaking the rice grain
  • You can use ready made Thai green curry  paste.One packet for one cup of rice.
