Parmesan-Crusted Chicken

Minija

 

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs (panko for extra crispiness)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or Italian seasoning
  • 2–3 tbsp olive oil or butter

Method

Prepare the Chicken

  1. Slice each chicken breast horizontally to make thinner pieces, or pound the breasts to an even thickness.
  2. Prepare three shallow bowls:
  3. Bowl 1: Mix the flour, salt, and black pepper.
  4. Bowl 2: Beat the egg with the minced garlic.
  5. Bowl 3: Mix the Parmesan cheese, breadcrumbs, and oregano or Italian seasoning.
  6. Coat each chicken piece in the flour mixture, dip it into the egg mixture, then press it into the Parmesan-breadcrumb mixture until well coated.

Pan-Fry

  1. Heat the olive oil or butter in a frying pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the coated chicken and cook for about 4–5 minutes on each side, until golden brown and crisp.
  3. Check that the chicken is fully cooked in the centre, with no pink remaining. The safe internal temperature is 74°C.

Optional Oven Finish

  1. For thicker pieces, transfer the pan-fried chicken to a preheated oven at 180°C and bake for another 8–10 minutes, or until fully cooked.

Notes

  • Panko breadcrumbs give the coating extra crunch.
  • Serve with mashed potatoes, garlic rice, and a fresh vegetable salad.
  • Adjust the cooking time according to the thickness of the chicken.
