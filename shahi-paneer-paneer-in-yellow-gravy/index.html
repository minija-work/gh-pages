Shahi Paneer (Paneer in Yellow Gravy)

Minija

A rich North Indian paneer curry with a golden, nutty gravy flavoured with almonds, cashews, saffron, and warming spices.

Medium • 40–45 mins • Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • 200 g paneer, cut into large cubes
  • 1½ cups water
  • 1 large onion or 3 small onions, chopped
  • 3 large garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 piece ginger, chopped
  • 2 tbsp cashew nuts
  • 2 tbsp almonds
  • 3 tsp ghee
  • 1 tsp shah jeera (caraway seeds)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt, to taste
  • 2 tbsp curd (yoghurt)
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Fresh cream, for garnish
  • Saffron strands, optional

Healthier alternative

  • Puréed soft tofu instead of fresh cream

Method

Prepare the nut and onion paste

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Add the chopped onion, garlic, ginger, cashews, and almonds.
  3. Boil for about 10 minutes, until the onions are soft.
  4. Switch off the heat. Strain the mixture, reserving the cooking stock.
  5. Remove the almond skins once cool enough to handle.
  6. Transfer the cooked ingredients to a blender, add about 2 tbsp of the reserved stock, and blend into a smooth paste.

Cook the gravy

  1. Heat the ghee in a wide saucepan.
  2. Add the shah jeera and sauté briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add the ground paste and cook, stirring, until it thickens slightly.
  4. Add the turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt.
  5. Gradually add some of the reserved stock, stirring to form a smooth gravy.
  6. In a small bowl, whisk the curd with the optional sugar until smooth.
  7. Lower the heat and gradually stir the curd mixture into the gravy.
  8. Cook gently for about 5 minutes.

Add the paneer

  1. Add the paneer cubes and simmer for another 5–7 minutes.
  2. If the gravy becomes too thick, add a little reserved stock or water. A small amount of whisked yoghurt can also be used.
  3. Garnish with fresh cream or puréed tofu and a pinch of saffron, if desired.

Notes

  • Remove the almond skins after boiling for a smoother gravy.
  • Add the curd over low heat and stir continuously to reduce the risk of curdling.
  • For the characteristic yellow colour, keep the chilli powder moderate and use the turmeric as specified.
  • Serve with roti, naan, chapati, or basmati rice.
