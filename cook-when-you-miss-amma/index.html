Cook when you miss Amma 

A compilation of recipes for when you miss Amma’s food

My dearest Chickoo and Baby ❤️

I made this little space for you, so that no matter where life takes you, you will never be far from home. Every recipe here carries memories—of our kitchen, our laughter, and the love we share as a family.

Whenever you miss home, I hope you come here, cook something, and feel close to me again. Food is my way of loving you, and that love will always stay with you.

It makes me so happy that Jenny and Anton enjoy my cooking too. Knowing that all of you share these meals fills my heart with so much joy ❤️

Take care of yourselves, eat well, and remember… Amma is always with you, in every dish you make.

With all my love,

Amma
