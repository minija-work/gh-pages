Tender Coconut Pudding

Minija

 

A refreshing, creamy pudding made with tender coconut water and soft coconut flesh.

Easy • 20 mins preparation • 2–3 hrs chilling • Dessert • Veg • Chilled

Ingredients

  • 2 cups tender coconut water
  • 1 cup tender coconut flesh (soft malai), chopped or lightly blended
  • 1 cup milk
  • 8–10 g china grass (agar-agar)
  • ½ cup water, for soaking the china grass
  • 4–5 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2–3 tbsp condensed milk (optional)

Method

Prepare the china grass

  1. Cut the china grass into small pieces, if necessary, and soak it in ½ cup water for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Heat gently until completely dissolved, stirring to remove any strands.

Prepare the milk mixture

  1. Heat the milk, sugar, and condensed milk in a saucepan.
  2. Stir until the sugar dissolves. There is no need to boil vigorously.
  3. Add the dissolved china grass to the hot milk and stir continuously for 2–3 minutes.

Add the coconut

  1. Switch off the heat and allow the mixture to cool slightly while remaining liquid.
  2. Stir in the tender coconut water and coconut flesh gently until combined.

Set the pudding

  1. Pour into a mould or glass dish.
  2. Allow it to cool at room temperature.
  3. Refrigerate for 2–3 hours, or until set.
  4. Cut into cubes or scoop gently and serve chilled.

Notes

  • Do not add the coconut water while the milk mixture is boiling vigorously.
  • For a clear jelly-style pudding, omit the milk and condensed milk and use coconut water, sugar, and agar-agar instead.
  • Adjust the sugar according to the natural sweetness of the coconut water.
