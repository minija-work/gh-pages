Easy Kerala Sambar

Minija

A quick and flavourful Kerala-style sambar made with lentils, tomatoes, shallots, and aromatic spices. Perfect for a simple everyday meal.

Easy • 35–40 mins • Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • ½ cup toor dal (pigeon peas)
  • 4 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 10 shallots, halved lengthwise
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 green chillies, slit lengthwise
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • ¾ tsp fenugreek powder
  • ½ tsp fenugreek seeds
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies, halved
  • 3 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1½ tbsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp tamarind juice
  • 1 tbsp oil, preferably coconut oil
  • 2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 4 cups water, divided as needed
  • 1 cup chopped coriander leaves
  • Curry leaves, a few sprigs
  • A pinch of asafoetida powder (optional)

Optional vegetables

  • Brinjal, potato, carrot, ash gourd, pumpkin, or raw banana

Method

  1. Cook the lentils: Wash the toor dal thoroughly. Pressure-cook with 1½ cups of water, half of the turmeric powder, and some salt for 2 whistles. Allow the pressure to release naturally, then set aside.
  2. Prepare the seasoning: Heat the oil in a pressure cooker. Add the mustard seeds and fenugreek seeds. Once the mustard seeds splutter, add the dried red chillies and curry leaves.
  3. Sauté the onions: Add the shallots, sliced onion, and slit green chillies. Sauté until the onions soften.
  4. Add the spices: Reduce the heat and add the coriander powder, red chilli powder, remaining turmeric powder, fenugreek powder, and asafoetida powder, if using. Sauté gently for about a minute, taking care not to burn the spices.
  5. Add the tomatoes: Stir in the tamarind juice, chopped tomatoes, remaining salt, and the rest of the water. Add any optional vegetables at this stage.
  6. Pressure-cook: Close the cooker and cook for 1 whistle. Allow the pressure to release naturally before opening.
  7. Add the dal: Stir in the cooked lentils and mix well. Bring to a gentle boil.
  8. Adjust the consistency: Simmer uncovered for about 5 minutes. Taste and adjust the salt, sourness, and spice level. Add a little water if the sambar is too thick.
  9. Garnish: Add the chopped coriander leaves and mix well.
  10. Serve hot with idli, dosa, or rice.

Notes

  • Coconut oil gives the sambar an authentic Kerala flavour.
  • Four large tomatoes provide plenty of tanginess, so adjust the tamarind juice according to your taste.
  • Add vegetables such as brinjal, potato, carrot, ash gourd, pumpkin, or raw banana for variety.
  • For a smoother texture, mash some of the cooked lentils before adding them to the sambar.
  • If using all 4 cups of water, add it gradually and adjust according to your preferred consistency.
