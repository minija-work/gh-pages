Chicken Fried Rice 🍗

Minija

A flavourful fried rice dish with chicken, vegetables, eggs, and soy sauce, perfect for a satisfying lunch or dinner.

Easy • 30–40 minutes • Non-vegetarian • Serves 3–4

Ingredients

  • Chicken breast – 500 g, cut into small pieces
  • Cooked white rice – 1½ cups, preferably leftover rice
  • Eggs – 2
  • Garlic – 3 cloves, minced
  • Ginger – 1 inch, minced
  • Oil – 1 tbsp
  • Carrot – ¼ cup, chopped
  • Celery – ¼ cup, chopped
  • Spring onions – 2, chopped
  • Chinese coriander – 1 sprig, chopped
  • Soy sauce – 1 tsp
  • Salt – ¼ tsp, or to taste
  • Black pepper – to taste
  • Water – a little, if needed

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat some oil in a pan and cook the chicken until golden and fully cooked. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, sauté the ginger and garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add the carrot and celery and cook until slightly tender.
  5. Add the white parts of the spring onions and sauté briefly.
  6. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry over high heat, breaking up any clumps. Add a little water if the rice is too dry.
  7. Add the soy sauce, salt, and black pepper. Mix well.
  8. Push the rice to one side of the pan. Crack the eggs into the empty side and scramble until cooked.
  9. Add the cooked chicken and combine everything thoroughly.
  10. Garnish with the green parts of the spring onions and chopped Chinese coriander. Serve hot.

Notes

  • Cold, leftover rice works best because the grains are less likely to become mushy.
  • If cooking rice specifically for this dish, cool it promptly and refrigerate it before use.
  • Ensure the chicken and eggs are fully cooked before serving.
  • Adjust the soy sauce and seasoning to taste.
