Caramel Custard Pudding

Minija

Ingredients

For the custard

  • 2 cups milk
  • 5 eggs
  • 10 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp vanilla essence
  • For the caramel
  • 8 tbsp sugar
  • A little water, just enough to help dissolve the sugar

Method

Prepare the Caramel

  1. Add the sugar and a little water to a saucepan.
  2. Heat over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and turns golden brown.
  3. Carefully pour the caramel into the pudding mould, tilting the mould to coat the bottom. Set aside.

Prepare the Custard

  1. In a bowl, beat the eggs gently.
  2. Add the sugar and mix until dissolved.
  3. Add the vanilla essence and milk. Mix well.
  4. Pour the custard mixture into the caramel-lined mould. For a smoother pudding, strain the mixture before pouring.

Steam the Pudding

  1. Place the mould in a steamer and steam over low to medium heat until the custard is set. The time will depend on the mould size.
  2. Insert a toothpick or skewer into the centre. It should come out mostly clean, with the custard set.
  3. Switch off the heat and allow the pudding to cool.
  4. Run a knife around the edges of the mould.
  5. Place a serving plate over the mould and carefully invert it.
  6. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled before serving.

Notes

  • Be careful when handling hot caramel, as it can cause severe burns.
  • Avoid beating too much air into the eggs to prevent bubbles in the pudding.
  • For a smooth texture, steam gently rather than allowing the water to boil vigorously.
