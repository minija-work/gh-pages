Kerala Parippu Curry with Toor Dal and Tomato

Minija

A homestyle Kerala-style dal curry made with toor dal, tomatoes, green chillies and coconut, with a comforting blend of spices. Delicious with rice, chapati or dosa.

Easy • 30–35 mins • Main Dish • Veg

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (thuvara parippu)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 small onion, chopped (optional)
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 cups water, or as needed

For the Coconut Paste

  • ½ cup grated coconut
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 green chilli (optional)
  • A little water for grinding

For Tempering

  • 1 tbsp coconut oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced (optional)
  • A pinch of asafoetida (optional)

Method

  1. Wash the toor dal thoroughly and place it in a pressure cooker.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes, green chillies, turmeric powder and 3 cups of water.
  3. Pressure-cook for about 4–5 whistles, or until the dal is soft and easy to mash. Allow the pressure to release naturally.
  4. Mash the dal and tomatoes together until well combined.
  5. Grind the grated coconut, cumin seeds and optional green chilli with a little water into a smooth paste.
  6. Add the coconut paste to the cooked dal. Add the red chilli powder and salt, then mix well.
  7. Simmer for 5–7 minutes, adding hot water if necessary to achieve your preferred consistency.
  8. For the tempering, heat coconut oil in a small pan. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  9. Add the cumin seeds, dried red chillies and curry leaves. If using shallots, fry them until golden brown. Add asafoetida if desired.
  10. Pour the tempering over the curry and mix gently.
  11. Serve hot with Kerala rice, chapati or dosa.

Notes

  • For a traditional Kerala flavour, use coconut oil and fresh curry leaves.
  • The coconut paste gives the curry a creamy texture. You can omit it for a simpler tomato dal.
  • Adjust the green chillies and red chilli powder to your taste.
  • A little ghee added while serving makes the curry richer.
