Soft Chapathi 🫓

Minija

 

Soft, wholesome Indian flatbreads made with whole-wheat flour. Perfect for everyday meals and delicious when served with curry, dal, or vegetable dishes.

Easy • 40–50 minutes • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Whole-wheat flour (atta) – 2 cups
  • Warm water – ¾ cup, or as needed
  • Oil or ghee – 1–2 tsp
  • Salt – a pinch (optional)
  • Extra dry flour – for rolling
  • Ghee – for brushing (optional)

Method

  1. Mix the whole-wheat flour and salt in a bowl.
  2. Gradually add warm water and knead into a soft dough.
  3. Add the oil or ghee and knead for 8–10 minutes until the dough becomes smooth and pliable.
  4. Cover and let the dough rest for 20–30 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into equal-sized balls.
  6. Dust each ball lightly with dry flour and roll it into a thin circle using a rolling pin.
  7. Heat a tawa over medium-high heat.
  8. Place a rolled chapathi on the hot tawa and cook until small bubbles appear.
  9. Flip the chapathi and cook the other side briefly.
  10. Flip again and gently press the surface with a spatula or clean cloth until it puffs up and cooks evenly.
  11. Remove from the tawa and brush with ghee, if desired.
  12. Keep the cooked chapathis covered in a clean container to retain their softness.

Notes

  • Use warm water to help make softer chapathies
  • The dough should be soft and pliable, not stiff or sticky.
  • Resting the dough helps relax the gluten and makes rolling easier.
  • Add water gradually, as the amount needed may vary depending on the flour.
  • Keep cooked chapathis covered with a clean cloth or in an insulated container to prevent them from drying out.
