Raw Papaya Pachadi

Minija

 

A creamy Kerala-style pachadi made with cooked raw papaya, coconut, green chillies and curd.

Easy • 20–25 mins • Side Dish • Veg

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups raw green papaya, peeled and finely chopped
  • ½ cup grated coconut
  • 4 green chillies
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (for grinding)
  • ½ cup curd (yoghurt)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)

For Tempering

  • 1 tsp coconut oil or other cooking oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • 1 sprig curry leaves

Method

  1. Place the chopped raw papaya in a pan with a little water and salt. Cook until soft but not mushy.
  2. Grind the grated coconut into a coarse paste. Add the green chillies and mustard seeds, then pulse two or three times until combined. Avoid grinding too smoothly.
  3. Add the coconut mixture to the cooked papaya and mix well. Cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  4. Switch off the heat and allow the mixture to cool slightly.
  5. Add the curd and mix gently until well combined. The pachadi should have a semi-thick, creamy consistency.
  6. Heat the oil in a small pan. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add the dried red chilli and curry leaves.
  7. Pour the tempering over the pachadi and mix gently.

Notes

  • Do not boil the pachadi after adding curd, as it may split.
  • Keep the consistency thick and creamy rather than watery.
  • Sugar is optional and can help balance the tanginess.
  • You can substitute the raw papaya with grated beetroot, raw mango, white melon or tomato. Cooking time may vary depending on the ingredient used.
