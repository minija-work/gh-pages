Marble Pudding

Minija

A beautiful two-tone milk pudding with delicate vanilla and chocolate swirls.

Easy • 20 mins preparation • 2–3 hrs chilling • Dessert • Veg • Chilled

Ingredients

  • 1 litre milk
  • 10 g china grass (agar-agar)
  • ½ cup water, for soaking the china grass
  • 5–6 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 3–4 tbsp condensed milk (optional, for richness)

Method

Prepare the china grass

  1. Cut the china grass into small pieces, if necessary, and soak it in ½ cup water for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Heat gently until completely dissolved, stirring to ensure no strands remain.

Prepare the milk mixture

  1. Heat the milk, sugar, and condensed milk in a saucepan.
  2. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Avoid boiling vigorously.
  3. Add the dissolved china grass to the hot milk, stirring continuously for 2–3 minutes.

Create the marble effect

  1. Divide the mixture equally between two bowls.
  2. Add the vanilla essence to one portion.
  3. Mix the cocoa powder with a little warm milk to form a smooth paste, then stir it into the second portion.
  4. Pour a ladleful of the white mixture into a mould, followed by some chocolate mixture.
  5. Continue alternating the two mixtures.
  6. Gently swirl a skewer or knife through the mixture to create a marble pattern. Do not overmix.

Set the pudding

  1. Allow it to cool at room temperature.
  2. Refrigerate for 2–3 hours, or until completely set.

Notes

  • Agar-agar powder can be used instead of china grass; follow the package instructions for the appropriate quantity.
  • Work quickly while the mixtures are still pourable, as agar-agar begins to set as it cools.
  • Do not over-swirl, or the marble pattern may disappear
