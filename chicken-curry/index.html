Chicken Curry

Minija

A flavourful Indian chicken curry made with bone-in chicken, freshly ground ginger-garlic paste, green chillies, and aromatic spices.

Easy • 35–40 mins • Main Course • Non-Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • 1 kg bone-in chicken
  • 3 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 2-inch piece of ginger
  • 7 green chillies
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp black pepper powder
  • 3 tbsp red chilli powder, adjusted to taste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp coriander powder
  • 2 sprigs curry leaves
  • 2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Oil of your choice
  • ½ cup water

Method

  1. Grind the green chillies, ginger, garlic, and fennel seeds into a coarse or smooth paste.
  2. Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Add the sliced onions and sauté until lightly browned.
  3. Add the ground paste and sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Add the chicken and curry leaves. Sauté well for a few minutes.
  5. Add the turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, black pepper powder, red chilli powder, and salt. Mix well and sauté until the chicken is coated with the spices.
  6. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they soften.
  7. Pour in ½ cup of water and mix well.
  8. Close the pressure cooker lid and cook for one whistle. Allow the pressure to release naturally before opening the cooker.
  9. Check that the chicken is fully cooked. If you prefer a thicker curry, simmer uncovered for a few minutes until the gravy reaches your desired consistency.
  10. Taste and adjust the salt and spice level. Serve hot with rice, chapati, appam, or dosa.

Notes

  • Adjust the red chilli powder and green chillies according to your preferred spice level.
  • The amount of water released by the chicken can vary, so add extra water only if needed.
  • Cooking time may vary depending on the pressure cooker and the size of the chicken pieces. Ensure the chicken is thoroughly cooked before serving.
  • For a richer flavour, allow the curry to rest for 5–10 minutes before serving.
