Fruit Custard

Minija

 

Creamy chilled custard loaded with fresh seasonal fruits.

Easy • 20 mins • Dessert • Veg • Chilled

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tbsp custard powder (vanilla)
  • 3–4 tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1–1.5 cups mixed fruits, chopped

Method

  1. Take ¼ cup cold milk and mix in the custard powder until smooth (no lumps).
  2. Bring the remaining milk to a gentle boil.
  3. Add sugar and stir until dissolved.
  4. Lower the heat and slowly pour in the custard mixture, stirring continuously.
  5. Cook on low heat for 3–5 minutes until it thickens.
  6. Turn off heat and let it cool.
  7. Refrigerate for 1–2 hours.
  8. Add chopped fruits just before serving and mix gently.

Notes

  • Best fruits to use: apple, banana, mango, grapes, pomegranate.
  • Avoid citrus fruits like orange or pineapple as they can curdle the custard or make it taste bitter.
  • Add fruits only after the custard is fully chilled to keep them fresh and prevent wateriness.
