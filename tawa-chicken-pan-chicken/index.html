Tawa Chicken (Pan Chicken)

Minija

A spicy Indian-style chicken dish cooked on a hot pan with tomatoes, fresh herbs, and aromatic spices.


Easy • 35–40 mins • Main Course • Non-Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into small chunks
  • 1 heaped tsp chopped ginger
  • 1 heaped tsp chopped garlic
  • 2 medium tomatoes, cut into thick slices
  • 3 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger, julienned
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 3 tbsp oil

Spice powders

  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ¾ tsp garam masala powder
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), roasted and crushed

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized chunks and drain well after washing.
  2. Heat the oil on a tawa or large frying pan.
  3. Add the chopped ginger and garlic. Fry over low heat for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the chicken and stir-fry over high heat for 3–4 minutes, until the chicken changes colour and most of the released moisture evaporates.
  5. Add the turmeric, red chilli, coriander, cumin, and garam masala powders along with the salt. Add a splash of water if needed and fry over medium heat for 3 minutes.
  6. Add the tomato slices and cook over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until they begin to soften.
  7. Add a little water, cover, and cook over low heat for about 10 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the tomatoes are soft.
  8. Remove the lid, add the crushed kasuri methi, and cook for another 2 minutes.
  9. Add the julienned ginger and chopped green chillies. Mix well.
  10. Add the chopped coriander leaves and stir-fry over medium heat for another 2 minutes.
  11. Serve hot with naan, roti, or chapati.

Notes

  • Boneless chicken thighs are ideal because they remain juicy during cooking.
  • Adjust the green chillies and red chilli powder to your preferred spice level.
  • Add water sparingly for a thick, semi-dry tawa-style preparation.
  • Ensure the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 74°C before serving.
