Palak Paneer (Paneer in Green Gravy)

Minija

Soft paneer cubes cooked in a vibrant spinach gravy with green chillies, garlic, tomatoes, and aromatic spices.

Easy • 35–40 mins • Main Course • Veg • Serve hot

Ingredients

For the spinach

  • 1 bunch palak (spinach), washed thoroughly

For the masala

  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 5 green chillies, finely chopped, or to taste
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece ginger
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped

For the curry

  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Water, as needed
  • 200 g paneer, cubed
  • Fresh cream or puréed tofu, for garnish

Method

Prepare the spinach

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil.
  2. Add the spinach leaves and blanch for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Drain immediately and transfer the spinach to a bowl of ice water to preserve its green colour.
  4. Drain again and set aside.

Prepare the masala

  1. Heat 1 tbsp butter or oil in a pan.
  2. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle briefly.
  3. Add the onion, green chillies, ginger, and garlic. Sauté until the onion softens.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Allow the mixture to cool slightly.
  6. Transfer it to a blender along with the blanched spinach and blend until smooth.

Cook the curry

  1. Heat the remaining butter or oil in the same pan.
  2. Add the turmeric, Kashmiri chilli powder, coriander powder, and garam masala.
  3. Sauté briefly over low heat, taking care not to burn the spices.
  4. Add the ground spinach mixture and a little water to achieve your preferred consistency.
  5. Add the salt and bring the gravy to a gentle boil.
  6. Simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.

Add the paneer

  1. Add the paneer cubes and mix gently.
  2. Cover and cook over low heat for another 5 minutes.
  3. Check the seasoning and adjust the salt if necessary.
  4. Garnish with fresh cream or puréed tofu and serve hot.

Notes

  • Blanching the spinach briefly and cooling it immediately helps preserve its bright green colour.
  • Adjust the green chillies according to your preferred spice level.
  • Avoid cooking the spinach gravy for too long after blending, as it may lose its vibrant colour.
  • For a lighter version, use puréed tofu instead of fresh cream.
  • Serve with naan, roti, chapati, or jeera rice.
