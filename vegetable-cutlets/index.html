Vegetable Cutlets 🥕🥔

Minija

 

Delicious, crispy vegetable cutlets made with mashed potatoes, colourful vegetables and aromatic spices. A perfect vegetarian snack for tea time or parties.

Medium • 40 mins • Snack • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 8 green beans, finely chopped
  • ½ cup green peas
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp finely chopped ginger and garlic
  • A few curry leaves, chopped
  • 1 small piece of beetroot (optional, for colour)
  • Finely chopped cauliflower (optional)

Spices

  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt, to taste
  • A handful of chopped coriander leaves

For Coating

  • 2 eggs, beaten, or maida slurry for an eggless version
  • Breadcrumbs, as needed

For Cooking

  • 2 tbsp oil, for sautéing
  • Oil, for frying or brushing

Method

1. Cook the Vegetables

  1. Steam or boil the carrot, beans and green peas until just tender. Do not overcook.
  2. Add finely chopped cauliflower, if desired.
  3. Add a small piece of beetroot for colour, if using.
  4. Drain all the water completely and set the vegetables aside.

2. Prepare the Masala

  1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped onions and curry leaves. Sauté until golden.
  3. Add the ginger, garlic and green chillies.
  4. Add the turmeric powder, red chilli powder, black pepper, garam masala and salt.
  5. Mix well and cook briefly.

3. Prepare the Filling

  1. Add the cooked vegetables to the masala.
  2. Add the mashed potatoes and mix thoroughly.
  3. Add the chopped coriander leaves.
  4. Taste and adjust the salt and spices.
  5. Cook briefly if necessary to remove excess moisture. The mixture should be dry and firm.
  6. Allow the mixture to cool completely.

4. Shape the Cutlets

  1. Take small portions of the mixture.
  2. Shape them into oval or round patties.

5. Coat the Cutlets

  1. Dip each cutlet in beaten egg or maida slurry.
  2. Roll in breadcrumbs until evenly coated.

6. Choose Your Cooking Method

Air Fryer

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Arrange the cutlets in a single layer.
  3. Brush or spray both sides lightly with oil.
  4. Air-fry for 12–15 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden.

Shallow Fry

  1. Heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Fry the cutlets until golden and crispy on both sides.

Deep Fry

  1. Heat oil over medium heat.
  2. Carefully add the cutlets and fry until crispy and golden.
  3. Drain on kitchen paper.

Note

  • Drain the vegetables thoroughly to prevent soggy cutlets.
  • If the mixture is too soft, add a little breadcrumbs to help bind it.
  • Serve hot with tomato ketchup or mint chutney.
