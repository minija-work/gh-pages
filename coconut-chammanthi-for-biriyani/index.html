Coconut Chammanthi for Biriyani

Minija

 

A fresh, tangy coconut chammanthi with green chillies and ginger, perfect as a flavourful accompaniment to biriyani.

Easy • 10 mins • Side Dish • Veg • Serve fresh

Ingredients

  • 1 cup freshly grated coconut
  • 2 green chillies, or to taste
  • 1 small piece of ginger
  • 1–2 tsp lemon juice or vinegar
  • Salt to taste
  • 2–4 tbsp water, as needed

Method

  1. Add the grated coconut, green chillies, ginger, salt, and lemon juice or vinegar to a mixer grinder.
  2. Add a little water and grind to a slightly coarse or smooth paste, according to your preference.
  3. Taste and adjust the salt, sourness, and spice level.
  4. Transfer to a serving bowl and serve fresh with biriyani.

Notes

  • Use lemon juice for a fresh citrus flavour or vinegar for a sharper tang.
  • Add water gradually to keep the chammanthi thick.
  • Fresh coconut gives the best flavour and texture.
