 Chicken Biriyani (Serves 4)

Minija

A fragrant Kerala-style biryani made with marinated chicken, basmati rice, and whole spices cooked together for rich layered flavor.


Medium • 60 mins • Non-Veg • One-pot / Dum option

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice – 2 cups    or jeerakasala rice for authentic Kerala taste)
  • Chicken – ½ kg
  • Turmeric powder – ½ tsp
  • Chilli powder – 1 tbsp(Adjust to taste)
  • Coriander powder – 1 tsp
  • Pepper powder – 1 tsp
  • Garam masala-1/2 tsp
  • Salt – 1 tsp (or to taste)
  • Curd (yogurt) – ¼ cup

Marinate the chicken with turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, pepper powder, salt, and curd. Keep aside for at least 30 minutes.

Vegetables & herbs

  • Onions – 2 large (sliced)
  • Tomatoes – 2 large (chopped)
  • Green chillies – 5
  • Ginger – 1-inch piece
  • Garlic – 10 cloves
  • Coriander leaves – 1 cup (chopped)
  • Mint leaves – ½ cup

Whole spices

  • Cloves – 3
  • Cardamom – 3
  • Cinnamon – ½-inch stick
  • Fennel seeds – ½ tsp
  • Mace – 1 (optional)

Other

  • Bay leaves – 1–2 (optional)
  • Lemon juice – 1 tbsp
  • Ghee – 1 tbsp
  • Water – 3¾ cups

Method

  1. Grind the ginger, garlic, green chillies, and whole spices into a paste.
  2. Heat oil and a little ghee in a pan. Sauté the sliced onions until soft and transparent.
  3. Add the ground paste and sauté for about 5 minutes until the raw smell disappears.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft and pulpy.
  5. Add the marinated chicken, along with coriander leaves and mint leaves. Mix well and sauté for about 7 minutes.
  6. Add 3¾ cups of water and bring to a boil. Then add the washed and drained rice.
  7. Add lemon juice, bay leaves (if using), 1 tbsp ghee, and adjust salt to taste.

Notes

Cooking options:

  • Pressure cooker: Cook for 1 whistle on medium flame.
  • Pot method: Cover with a lid and cook on medium flame until the water is absorbed and the rice is done.

Optional garnish:

  • Top with chopped coriander leaves, fried onions, cashews,

Layering (Dum method – optional):

  • You can cook the rice separately until 75% done( add salt to taste and lime juice 1 tsp)then layer it over 75% cooked chicken.    Cover tightly and cook on very low heat for 20 minutes (place a fry pan ( cast iron pan is better) underneath to avoid burning). Add garnishes while layering. And sprinkle a pinch of turmeric mixed in 1 tbs water for colour
