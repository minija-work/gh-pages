Kerala Avial

Minija

A classic Kerala mixed-vegetable dish coated in a coconut and cumin paste, finished with curd and fragrant coconut oil.

Medium • 35–40 mins • Side Dish • Veg • Serve warm

Ingredients

For the vegetables

  • 1 carrot, cut into medium-thick long strips
  • 1 raw banana, cut into strips
  • 1 drumstick, cut into pieces
  • 1 potato, cut into strips
  • 4 long beans, cut into pieces
  • ½ cup elephant yam (chena), cut into strips
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • A little water

For the coconut paste

  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 2–3 green chillies
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 shallots

For finishing

  • ½ cup curd (yoghurt)
  • 1–2 tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 sprig curry leaves

Method

  1. Cut all the vegetables into similar-sized, medium-thick strips.
  2. Place the vegetables in a wide pan with turmeric powder, salt, and a little water.
  3. Cover and cook until the vegetables are tender but still hold their shape.
  4. Grind the coconut, green chillies, cumin seeds, and shallots into a coarse paste.
  5. Add the coconut paste to the cooked vegetables. Mix gently and cook over low heat for 2–3 minutes.
  6. Reduce the heat further and gently mix in the curd.
  7. Warm through without allowing the avial to boil after adding the curd.
  8. Drizzle coconut oil over the top and add the curry leaves.
  9. Mix lightly, switch off the heat, and serve.

Notes

  • Avial should be thick, not watery.
  • Avoid overcooking the vegetables.
  • Coconut oil gives the dish its traditional Kerala flavour.
  • For a vegan version, omit the curd and use raw mango for tanginess.
