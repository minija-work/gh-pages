Kerala Meen Pathiri 🐟

Minija

A traditional Kerala-style steamed rice-flour pathiri filled with spicy fish masala and coconut.

Medium • 60–75 minutes • Non-vegetarian

Ingredients

For the fish filling

  • Fish (tuna, mackerel, or sardine) – 250 g
  • Onion – 2 small, finely chopped
  • Green chillies – 2–3
  • Ginger-garlic paste – 1 tbsp
  • Curry leaves – a few sprigs
  • Grated coconut – ½ cup
  • Oil – as needed

Spices

  • Turmeric powder – ¼ tsp
  • Red chilli powder – 1 tsp
  • Black pepper powder – ½ tsp
  • Fennel seeds – ½ tsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Lemon juice – 1 tsp (optional)

For the pathiri dough

  • Rice flour – 1½ cups
  • Water – 1½ cups
  • Salt – to taste
  • Coconut oil – 1 tsp

Method

1. Prepare the fish

  1. Cook the fish with a little salt and turmeric powder.
  2. Remove all bones and shred the fish.

2. Prepare the filling

  1. If desired, coarsely grind the grated coconut with the green chillies and fennel seeds.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add the onions and curry leaves and sauté until golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until fragrant.
  4. Add the turmeric and red chilli powders.
  5. Add the shredded fish and the ground coconut mixture.
  6. Season with salt and black pepper. Cook until the mixture is fairly dry.
  7. Add lemon juice, if using, and allow the filling to cool.

3. Make the dough

  1. Bring the water, salt, and coconut oil to a boil.
  2. Reduce the heat and add the rice flour, mixing well.
  3. Turn off the heat and mix until the flour comes together.
  4. When cool enough to handle but still warm, knead into a soft dough.

4. Shape the pathiri

  1. Divide the dough into equal-sized balls.
  2. Roll each ball into a thin disc using a banana leaf or lightly greased foil.
  3. Spread the fish filling over one disc, leaving a small border around the edges.
  4. Cover with another disc and seal the edges firmly.

5. Steam

  1. Place the filled pathiri in a steamer.
  2. Steam until the rice-flour dough is fully cooked, approximately 10–15 minutes, depending on thickness.
  3. Allow it to rest briefly before serving.

Notes

  • Remove all fish bones carefully before preparing the filling.
  • Keep the filling dry so the pathiri does not become soggy.
  • Adjust the green chillies and chilli powder to your taste.
