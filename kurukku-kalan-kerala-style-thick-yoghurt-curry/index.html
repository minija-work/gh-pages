Kurukku Kalan (Kerala-Style Thick Yoghurt Curry)

Minija

 

A traditional Kerala sadya curry made with raw banana, elephant yam, coconut, and thick curd, simmered until rich and creamy.

Medium • 40–50 mins • Side Dish • Veg • Serve warm

Ingredients

  • 1 raw banana, peeled and cubed
  • 1 cup elephant yam (chena), cubed
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper powder
  • Salt to taste
  • A little water
  • 1½ cups thick curd (yoghurt)

For grinding

  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2–3 green chillies

For tempering

  • 2 tbsp coconut oil or other cooking oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • ¼ tsp fenugreek seeds
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1 sprig curry leaves

Method

  1. Peel and cube the raw banana and elephant yam.
  2. Place them in a pan with turmeric powder, red chilli powder, black pepper powder, salt, and a little water.
  3. Cover and cook until the vegetables are tender and most of the water has evaporated.
  4. Grind the grated coconut, cumin seeds, and green chillies into a smooth paste.
  5. Add the paste to the cooked vegetables and simmer for 3–4 minutes.
  6. Beat the curd until smooth. Lower the heat and gradually add the curd, stirring gently.
  7. Cook over very low heat until the curry thickens. Do not allow it to boil vigorously after adding the curd.
  8. For the tempering, heat coconut oil in a small pan.
  9. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter. Add fenugreek seeds, dried red chillies, and curry leaves.
  10. Pour the tempering over the kalan and mix gently.

Notes

  • Traditional kalan is thick, slightly sour, and peppery.
  • Use thick curd for the best consistency.
  • The curry thickens further as it cools.
  • Serve with rice as part of a Kerala sadya.
