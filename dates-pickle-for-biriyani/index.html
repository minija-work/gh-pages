Dates Pickle for Biriyani

Minija

 

A sweet, spicy, and tangy dates pickle that pairs beautifully with the rich flavours of Kerala-style biriyani.

Easy • 20 mins • Condiment • Veg • Serve as a side

Ingredients

  • 150 g seedless dates
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1-inch piece of ginger, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 1½ tbsp red chilli powder, adjusted to taste
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Soak the seedless dates in the vinegar until they soften.
  2. Grind the dates and vinegar into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat the oil in a pan and add the mustard seeds. Allow them to splutter.
  4. Add the chopped ginger, garlic, and green chillies. Sauté until the raw smell disappears.
  5. Add the salt and red chilli powder. Sauté briefly over low heat, taking care not to burn the chilli powder.
  6. Add the dates-vinegar paste and mix well.
  7. Cook over low heat for a few minutes, stirring continuously, until the pickle thickens slightly.
  8. Switch off the heat and allow the pickle to cool before serving.

Notes

  • Adjust the chilli powder according to your preferred spice level.
  • The dates provide natural sweetness, while the vinegar adds tanginess.
  • Use a clean, dry spoon when serving and refrigerate leftovers in a clean, airtight container.
  • This pickle is delicious with biriyani, ghee rice, and other Indian rice dishes.
