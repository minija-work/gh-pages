Prawns Mango Curry

Minija

Tangy mango and prawns cooked in a spicy coconut-based Kerala curry.

Medium • 35 mins • Non-Veg • Curry • Coastal

Ingredients

  • 1/2 kg prawns
  • 1 mango, cut into pieces
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 5 green chillies
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 1/2 tbsp chilli powder
  • 2 1/2 tsp salt
  • A few curry leaves
  • 5 small onions
  • 1/2 cup grated coconut
  • Oil, as needed
  • 1/2tsp Mustard seeds

Method

  1. Cook mango pieces, prawns, ginger, green chillies, turmeric powder, chilli powder, and salt together until the mango becomes soft.
  2. Grind grated coconut with 2 small onions into a smooth paste.
  3. Add the coconut paste to the curry and cook for about 3 minutes.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and add mustard seeds. Let them splutter.
  5. Add chopped onions and fry until brown. Add curry leaves.
  6. Pour the tempering into the curry and mix well.
  7. Serve hot.

Notes

  • Adjust spice levels based on preference.
  • You can substitute prawns with other fish to make fish mango curry.
