Kerala Tuna Cutlets 🐟

Minija

 

Traditional Kerala-style tuna cutlets made with canned tuna, mashed potatoes, curry leaves and aromatic spices. Crispy on the outside and soft and flavourful inside.

Easy • 35 mins • Snack • Non-Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 can tuna, drained well
  • 2 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1–2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp chopped ginger
  • A few curry leaves, chopped

Spices

  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp black pepper powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp fennel powder (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • A handful of chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)

For Coating

  • 1–2 eggs, beaten, or flour slurry for an eggless version
  • Breadcrumbs, as needed

For Cooking

  • 2 tbsp oil, for sautéing
  • Oil, for frying or brushing

Method

1. Prepare the Tuna

  1. Drain the canned tuna thoroughly.
  2. Flake it well with a fork, ensuring there is no excess liquid.

2. Prepare the Masala

  1. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped onions and curry leaves. Sauté until golden.
  3. Add the ginger and green chillies.
  4. Add the turmeric powder, red chilli powder, black pepper, garam masala and fennel powder, if using.
  5. Mix well and cook briefly.

3. Add the Tuna

  1. Add the flaked tuna to the masala.
  2. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring until the mixture is dry.
  3. Add lemon juice, if desired.

4. Combine with Potatoes

  1. Add the mashed potatoes and mix thoroughly.
  2. Add the chopped coriander leaves.
  3. Taste and adjust the salt and spices.
  4. Cook briefly if needed to remove excess moisture.
  5. Allow the mixture to cool completely. It should be dry and firm.

5. Shape the Cutlets

  1. Take small portions of the mixture.
  2. Shape them into oval or round patties.

6. Coat the Cutlets

  1. Dip each cutlet in beaten egg or flour slurry.
  2. Roll in breadcrumbs until evenly coated.

7. Choose Your Cooking Method

Air Fryer

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180°C.
  2. Arrange the cutlets in a single layer.
  3. Brush or spray both sides lightly with oil.
  4. Air-fry for 12–15 minutes, flipping halfway through, until golden and crispy.

Shallow Fry

  1. Heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Fry the cutlets until golden brown on both sides.

Deep Fry

  1. Heat oil over medium heat.
  2. Fry the cutlets until crispy and golden.
  3. Drain on kitchen paper.

Note

  • Drain the tuna thoroughly to prevent the mixture from becoming soggy.
  • Fennel powder adds a lovely traditional Kerala bakery flavour.
  • Serve hot with tomato ketchup or mint chutney.
  • Appetisers/snacks
