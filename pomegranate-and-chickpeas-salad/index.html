Pomegranate and Chickpeas Salad 🥗

Minija

A colourful and nutritious salad combining chickpeas, pomegranate, green peas and raw mango with a refreshing hint of lime.

Easy • 15 mins • Salad • Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas
  • ½ cup pomegranate seeds
  • ½ cup cooked green peas (do not overcook)
  • 1 large green chilli, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp grated raw green mango
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp grated coconut
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Add the cooked chickpeas and green peas to a large bowl.
  2. Add the pomegranate seeds, chopped green chilli and grated raw mango.
  3. Add the black pepper, chopped coriander leaves and grated coconut.
  4. Pour in the lime juice and add salt to taste.
  5. Mix all the ingredients well.
  6. Serve immediately.

Note

  • Do not overcook the green peas, as they should retain their texture.
  • Adjust the green chilli and lime juice according to your taste.
