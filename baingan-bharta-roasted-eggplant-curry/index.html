Baingan Bharta (Roasted Eggplant Curry)

Minija

 

A North Indian-style roasted eggplant curry with onions, tomatoes and aromatic spices, finished with fresh coriander.

Easy • 40–50 mins • Main Dish/Side Dish • Veg

Ingredients

  • 1 large baingan (eggplant/brinjal)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1–2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped, for garnish
  • A little ghee (optional)

Method

  1. Wash the eggplant and pat it dry. Apply a little oil over the skin.
  2. Roast over an open flame: Turn the eggplant regularly until the skin is charred and the flesh is soft.
    Alternatively, roast in the oven: Preheat the oven to 200°C and roast for about 30 minutes, or until the eggplant is completely soft. Cooking time will vary with its size.
  3. Allow the eggplant to cool. Peel off the charred skin and mash the flesh. Set aside.
  4. Heat the oil in a pan. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle briefly. Add the asafoetida.
  5. Add the chopped onion and sauté until golden brown.
  6. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook until the raw smell disappears.
  7. Add the green chillies and stir.
  8. Add the turmeric powder, red chilli powder and coriander powder. Mix well over low heat.
  9. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until soft and the oil begins to separate.
  10. Add the mashed eggplant and salt. Mix thoroughly.
  11. Cook for another 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the flavours combine.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves. Drizzle with a little ghee, if desired, before serving.

Notes

  • Roasting the eggplant over an open flame gives the curry its characteristic smoky flavour.
  • Choose a large, fleshy eggplant for the best texture.
  • Serve hot with chapati, roti, naan or rice.
  • Ghee is optional but adds richness.
