Curd Rice (Yoghurt Rice) 🥣

Minija

A comforting South Indian dish made with soft rice, creamy yoghurt, and a fragrant tempering of mustard seeds, curry leaves, and spices.

Easy • 20–25 minutes • Vegetarian

Ingredients

For the rice

  • Cooked rice – 1 cup, slightly mashed
  • Plain yoghurt – 1½ cups
  • Milk – ¼ cup (optional)
  • Sour cream – 1 tbsp (optional)
  • Salt – to taste

For the tempering

  • Oil – as needed
  • Mustard seeds – ½ tsp
  • Urad dal – 1 tsp
  • Dried red chilli – 1
  • Curry leaves – a few
  • Asafoetida (hing) – a pinch
  • Ginger – a small piece, finely chopped
  • Green chilli – 1, finely chopped

Optional additions

  • Grated carrot
  • Chopped cucumber
  • Pomegranate seeds

Method

  1. Place the cooked rice in a bowl and mash it slightly.
  2. Allow the rice to cool until it is no longer hot.
  3. Add the yoghurt and salt. Mix until creamy.
  4. Add the milk if you prefer a softer consistency. Stir in the sour cream, if using.
  5. Heat oil in a small pan.
  6. Add the mustard seeds and allow them to splutter.
  7. Add the urad dal and dried red chilli. Fry until the dal turns golden.
  8. Add the curry leaves, ginger, green chilli, and asafoetida. Sauté briefly.
  9. Pour the tempering over the rice and yoghurt mixture. Mix well.
  10. Add grated carrot, cucumber, or pomegranate seeds if desired. Serve cool or at room temperature.

Notes

  • Use fresh yoghurt or slightly sour yoghurt according to your preference.
  • Adding milk helps prevent the rice from becoming too thick.
  • For a refreshing finish, garnish with pomegranate seeds.
  • Serve with mango pickle or spicy potatoes.
