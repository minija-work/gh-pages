Dosa

Minija

 

A South Indian breakfast favourite with a crisp golden exterior and a soft centre, perfect with chutney, sambar, or potato masala.

Medium • 30 mins (+ soaking & fermentation) • Veg • Breakfast Favourite

Ingredients

  • 2 cups idli rice
  • 1 cup urad dal (whole)
  • 1/2 cup cooked leftover rice
  • 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Salt, to taste
  • Water, as needed

Method

  1. Wash rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds היט and soak separately. Add about 3 cups of water to the urad dal while soaking. Soak for 6 hours.
  2. Drain the rice and grind it using some of the soaked urad dal water to a smooth batter.
  3. Grind urad dal with cooked rice using the soaked water until smooth and fluffy.
  4. Combine both batters and mix well with your hand. Add salt to taste.
  5. Cover and ferment overnight until the batter rises and becomes airy.
  6. Heat a non-stick or cast-iron tawa and lightly grease with oil.
  7. Pour a ladle of batter and spread in a circular motion from the center.
  8. Cook on medium-low heat until the edges lift and the bottom turns golden.
  9. Sprinkle a little water if the pan becomes too hot.
  10. Remove and serve hot.

Note

  • Adding cooked rice or a handful of poha while grinding helps make crisp dosas.
  • To prevent sticking, rub a cut onion with a little oil on the tawa before making the first dosa.
  • A well-fermented batter will float when dropped in water.
  • If using store-bought batter, leave it outside the fridge for about an hour before use for better results
