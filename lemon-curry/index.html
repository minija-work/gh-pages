Lemon Curry

Minija

 

A tangy and spicy lemon curry with mustard seeds, fenugreek and curry leaves, balanced with a pinch of sugar.

Easy • 15–20 mins • Side Dish/Pickle • Veg

Ingredients

  • 1 lemon
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1½ tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp fenugreek seeds
  • ½ tsp salt, or to taste
  • A few curry leaves
  • 3 tbsp water
  • A pinch of sugar

Method

  1. Wash the lemon and cut it into small pieces, removing any seeds.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan. Add the mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add the fenugreek seeds and sauté briefly, taking care not to burn them.
  4. Add the curry leaves.
  5. Reduce the heat to low and add the red chilli powder. Stir well so it does not burn.
  6. Add the chopped lemon and green chillies. Mix thoroughly.
  7. Add the salt and a pinch of sugar.
  8. Pour in the water and simmer until the mixture thickens slightly.
  9. Switch off the heat and allow the curry to cool before serving.

Notes

  • Adjust the chilli powder, salt and sugar to balance the lemon's sourness.
  • Avoid overcooking the lemon, as it can become bitter.
  • Store in a clean, airtight container in the refrigerator.
